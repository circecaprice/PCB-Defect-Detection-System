STM32 firmware contract (what to implement next)
The STM32 tab assumes a small ASCII protocol over USB CDC. Minimum firmware for testing:
Command (RX from PC)ActionTX responsePING\n—PONG\nECHO <text>\n—<text>\nLED <ch> <0-255>\nSet PWM duty on TIMx channelOK\nLED ALL <0-255>\nSet all channelsOK\n
For your USB-CDC ↔ UART loopback test, the simplest firmware just echoes every byte received on USB back out, OR forwards USB→UART1 TX (PA9) and UART1 RX (PA10)→USB. You can then short PA9↔PA10 and verify everything you type in the "Send" box comes back in the console.
What this gives you

Rail tab — your existing WeMacro UI, unchanged in behavior
Camera tab — live view (threaded, non-blocking), gain, exposure (µs), binning 1/2/4, RAW8/RAW16, sensor temp polling, cooler on/off with target temp, single-shot capture ("Trigger Shutter")
STM32 tab — port + baud selection, raw text send, hex send (HEX 01 02 FF), PING button, 4 LED PWM sliders + master slider, scrolling RX console
